Vision
A future where every U.S. Navy
Submariner is recognized, supported,
and cared for, and where the health
consequences of submarine service are
fully understood, acknowledged, and
never ignored.
We envision a future where:

•

Scientific research advances
understanding of submarine servicerelated health conditions.

•

Every eligible Submariner receives
timely access to earned healthcare,
benefits, and compensation.

•

Policymakers recognize the unique
occupational hazards of submarine
service.

•

Veterans and their families never face
these challenges alone.
