About the Submariners’ Advocacy Group
The Submariners' Advocacy Group (SAG) is a nonprofit organization founded by U.S. Navy Submariners to serve, support, and advocate for every individual who has
earned the right to wear the coveted Submarine Warfare Insignia—better known throughout the Fleet as the Dolphins.
Every member of SAG's leadership has completed the U.S. Navy's demanding submarine qualification process and understands firsthand the unique challenges,
sacrifices, and lifelong bonds that define service in the Silent Service. Our shared experiences provide the perspective, credibility, and commitment necessary to advocate
effectively on behalf of our submarine community.
Unlike traditional membership organizations, SAG recognizes that earning your Dolphins is membership enough. There are no applications, dues, or eligibility
requirements beyond having been qualified in submarines. If you have served as a U.S. Navy Submariner, you are already part of the community we exist to represent.
SAG is dedicated to improving the lives of Submariners through advocacy, education, collaboration, and awareness. We work to ensure that the unique occupational and
health concerns associated with submarine service receive the attention they deserve while building partnerships with healthcare professionals, researchers, veterans'
organizations, policymakers, and other stakeholders who share our commitment to those who served beneath the sea.
Our mission extends beyond today's veterans. We advocate for active-duty Submariners, retirees, and future generations of undersea warriors by promoting research,
supporting informed public policy, and encouraging greater understanding of the extraordinary service performed by the men and women of the United States Submarine
Force.
The Silent Service has always been defined by professionalism, sacrifice, and quiet excellence. SAG exists to ensure that same commitment continues long after the
watch has ended—serving as a trusted voice for our Brothers and Sisters of the submarine community wherever they may live.
