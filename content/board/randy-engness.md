Board of Directors
Randy Engness
Randy Engness serves in a leadership role within the Submariners’ Advocacy Group (SAG), supporting the organization’s mission to advocate for U.S. Navy
Submariners, veterans, and their families. As a member of the Silent Service community, Randy brings operational submarine experience, professional
leadership, and a continued commitment to supporting fellow Submariners and the broader veteran community.
Throughout his naval career, Randy served in the United States Navy Submarine Force, where he gained firsthand experience operating within the
demanding and highly disciplined environment unique to undersea warfare. His service in the Silent Service reinforced the values of accountability,
teamwork, professionalism, and mission-focused leadership that continue to guide both his professional and advocacy efforts today.
Following his military service, Randy continued building upon the technical and leadership foundation established during his submarine career through
continued professional development and organizational support roles. His experience reflects a strong commitment to disciplined service, operational
excellence, and supporting mission-driven organizations.
Within SAG, Randy supports initiatives focused on advocacy, awareness, organizational development, and strengthening support networks for current and
former Submariners. He remains dedicated to increasing awareness surrounding the long-term impacts associated with submarine service while
supporting efforts to ensure Submariners receive the recognition, advocacy, and support they deserve.
Randy’s continued dedication to the Silent Service community reflects SAG’s core values of integrity, professionalism, and service. Through his work with
SAG, he remains committed to supporting those who have served beneath the sea and advocating for the long-term well-being of Submariners and their
families.
