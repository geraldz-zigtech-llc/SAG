Board of Directors
Bruce Tarleton
Bruce Tarleton is a founding member of the Submariners’ Advocacy Group (SAG) and serves as a leading voice in the organization’s mission to
advocate for U.S. Navy Submariners, veterans, and their families. A proud member of the Silent Service community, Bruce has dedicated himself to
increasing awareness surrounding the long-term health impacts and unseen hazards associated with submarine service.
Throughout his naval career, Bruce served in the United States Navy Submarine Force, where he gained firsthand experience operating within the
demanding and highly disciplined environment unique to undersea warfare. His service in the Silent Service instilled the values of professionalism,
accountability, resilience, and mission-focused teamwork that continue to guide his advocacy efforts today.
Bruce’s personal experiences following his submarine service became a driving force behind his commitment to supporting fellow Submariners and
raising awareness surrounding the long-term health concerns affecting the undersea warfare community. Recognizing the need for stronger advocacy,
improved awareness, and increased support for those exposed to the unique environmental conditions associated with submarine operations, he
became one of the driving forces behind the formation and growth of SAG.
As a founding member of SAG, Bruce has helped support organizational outreach, veteran engagement, awareness initiatives, and advocacy efforts
focused on ensuring the experiences and sacrifices of Submariners receive the attention and recognition they deserve. His continued dedication
reflects SAG’s core values of integrity, professionalism, and service to the Silent Service community.
Bruce remains committed to supporting current and former Submariners while helping advance national awareness regarding the long-term health and
exposure concerns associated with life beneath the sea.
