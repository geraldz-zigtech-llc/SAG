Board of Directors
Jerry Lainhart
Jerry D. Lainhart serves on the Board of Directors for the Submariners’ Advocacy Group (SAG), supporting the organization’s mission to advocate for
U.S. Navy Submariners, veterans, and their families. A qualified Submariner and proud member of the Silent Service community, Jerry brings
operational submarine experience, professional leadership, and a lifelong commitment to service to SAG’s growing national advocacy efforts.
Jerry qualified in submarines aboard USS CAVALLA (SSN 684) in March 1990 while serving as an Electronics Technician. During his assignment
aboard CAVALLA from 1989 through 1993, he supported the demanding operational mission requirements associated with fast-attack submarine
operations during a critical period in modern naval history. His time in the Submarine Force instilled the values of professionalism, accountability,
teamwork, and mission-focused service that continue to guide him today.
Following his naval service, Jerry pursued higher education and professional development, earning a Bachelor of Science degree from Brigham
Young University in 1999 and a Master of Science degree from the University of Wisconsin–Platteville in 2005. He later achieved certification as a
Project Management Professional (PMP), further strengthening his leadership and organizational management expertise.
Jerry’s combined background in submarine operations, technical systems, higher education, and project management provides valuable perspective
in support of SAG’s mission and organizational growth. His experience reflects a continued commitment to professional excellence, disciplined
leadership, and supporting fellow members of the Submarine community.
As part of SAG’s leadership team, Jerry supports initiatives focused on advocacy, awareness, and strengthening support networks for current and
former Submariners. His dedication reflects SAG’s core values of integrity, professionalism, and service to the Silent Service community.
Jerry currently resides in Thayne, Wyoming, with his wife, LaDawna.
