Why Submariners Advocacy Group?

We are an association of veteran Submariners, all having earned and received the US Navy designation of “Qualified in Submarines”
(SS). All our Board Members, Founders, future Advocates, and first contact associates have been there and done that on US Navy
submarines, wherever “there” was and whatever “that” may have been. We have undertaken the arduous Submarine Qualification
Program, been tested and tried by our shipmates, and found to be worthy of the moniker “Submariner.”
All advocates of the Submariners’ Advocacy Group (SAG) are committed to being a voice for all of our Brother and Sister US Navy
Submariners. Submariners share a very unique bond forged in the depths of the oceans. We cannot adequately describe the experience
to those who have never experienced life inside a submarine. Often, some aspects, knowledge, and specifics can never be shared with
anyone outside of those who have served in the Silent Service. There is no membership application to fill out. You are eligible to be a
part of the SAG because of the Dolphins you wear on your chest, you are “Qualified in Submarines,” you are a Submariner. If you are a
Submariner you are automatically included.
SAG will be engaging the services of our future Affiliated Healthcare Professional Partners. These partners will be specialists in diving
deeper into health issues that may be associated with our duties onboard US Navy submarines, specifically, Pulmonologists,
Cardiologists, Oncologists, Dermatologists, Toxicologists, Ear-Nose-Throat specialists, Neurologists, and more. While these partners
may not have served on submarines, SAG will provide them with all the information necessary to assist them in assisting Submariners.
SAG will seek Affiliated Research Partners to analyze the nationwide Submariner Registry and the data collected from US Submariners
throughout the nation or wherever they may live. These Research Partners will be institutions with outstanding reputations and worldclass research facilities. Further, SAG will be seeking partnerships with Veteran Service Organizations to leverage their services to
assist veterans. This will include lobbying to get legislation passed addressing proper VA services, recognition of submarine-connected
disabilities, and additional research conducted to better care for and protect all Submariners, both veterans and active duty.
These sound like lofty, “pie in the sky” goals, and they are. Yes, this won’t happen overnight and will require concerted efforts from all of
the SAG team, but nothing will stop SAG from becoming the strongest voice on behalf of our Brothers and Sisters who voluntarily
served in the US Submarine Force. We are the Elite of the Fleet and deserve elite recognition and services.
