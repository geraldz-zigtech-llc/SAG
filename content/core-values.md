Core Values
Integrity. Professionalism. Courage.
Three principles that define who we are
and guide everything we do.
•

Integrity. We lead with honesty,
ethics, and accountability. Our
advocacy is grounded in facts,
transparency, and trust.

•

Professionalism. We pursue
excellence through discipline,
credibility, and respect. Every action
reflects our commitment to those we
serve.

•

Courage. We challenge injustice,
confront difficult truths, and pursue
meaningful change for every U.S.
Navy Submariner.
