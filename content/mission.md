Mission
To advocate for the health, recognition,
and care of all U.S. Navy Submariners
through scientific research, public
education, and legislative advocacy.
We are committed to:
•

Driving research into submarinerelated atmospheric contaminants and
other occupational exposures

•

Supporting legislation that protects
and benefits Submariners

•

Educating veterans, families,
healthcare providers, and
policymakers

•

Ensuring every Submariner has
access to information, advocacy, and
a voice when facing serviceconnected health challenges.
