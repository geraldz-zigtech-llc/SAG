Our Goals
The Submariners' Advocacy Group is committed to advancing the health, well-being, and recognition of the U.S. Navy submarine community through focused, long-term
initiatives that create meaningful and lasting impact.

Advocate for the Submarine Community
Promote legislation, public policy, and institutional change that improve healthcare, benefits, recognition, and support for U.S. Navy Submariners, their families, and
survivors.

Advance Research
Support the development of a nationwide Submariner registry, encourage scientific and medical research, and collaborate with leading academic and research institutions
to better understand the long-term effects of submarine service.

Educate and Inform
Develop educational resources for Veteran Service Officers, healthcare professionals, policymakers, and the public to increase understanding of submarine operations,
occupational exposures, and the unique challenges faced by those who served beneath the sea.

Build Strategic Partnerships
Establish collaborative relationships with veterans organizations, military commands, healthcare providers, universities, researchers, and government agencies to
strengthen advocacy efforts and expand available resources.

Support Submariners and Their Families
Provide trusted information, guidance, and advocacy for Submariners, their families, and surviving family members while fostering a lifelong community built on shared
service and mutual support.
