Chairman of the Board

Stanley Martinez

Stanley Martinez is a founding member and currently serves as Chairman of the Board for the Submariners’ Advocacy Group (SAG), helping lead the organization’s mission to advocate for U.S. Navy Submariners, veterans, and their families. As one of SAG’s founding leaders, Stanley has played a central role in helping shape the organization’s strategic direction, outreach efforts, and growing national advocacy presence within the veteran and submarine communities.

A proud supporter of the Silent Service community, Stanley has dedicated himself to increasing awareness surrounding the unique operational demands and long-term health concerns associated with submarine duty. He remains deeply committed to educating current and former Submariners about the potential impacts of toxic exposures, radiation, and other unseen hazards associated with service beneath the sea, while advocating for improved awareness, recognition, and support for affected veterans.

In addition to his leadership role within SAG, Stanley has maintained a long and distinguished record of service and advocacy within the Veterans of Foreign Wars (VFW). In the state of Utah, he served as State Commander from 2017 to 2018 and later served on the VFW National Legislative Committee from 2020 through 2024. His leadership within the veteran community reflects a continued commitment to supporting veterans at both the state and national levels.

Stanley is a Gold Legacy Life Member of the Veterans of Foreign Wars and the VFW National Home. He is also a Life Member of the Disabled American Veterans (DAV), Fleet Reserve Association (FRA), and the American Legion.

Further reflecting his commitment to national service, Stanley currently serves as a Presidential Appointee and lifetime board member for the United States Selective Service System.

Stanley holds a bachelor’s degree in Medical Management and continues to dedicate his time and leadership toward advancing veteran advocacy, organizational growth, and support for the Submarine Force community.
