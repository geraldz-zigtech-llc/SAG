Donation Program

Support Our Submariners

Why do we ask for donations? Well, quite frankly, it’s a bit expensive to meet with key members of important organzations to introduce them to the needs of Submariners.

Transparancy

What exactly are we spending your donations on?

  • Travel: Providing resources and assistance to families, ensuring they have the support they need while their loved ones are away.
  • Business Operations: Funding educational programs and training to enhance the skills and career opportunities of submariners.

Real Stories, Real Impact

Do you have a story of how the Submariners’ Advocacy Group has helped you or someone you know? Share it with us at public-affairs@submarinersadvocacygroup.org.

How to Donate

We have partnered with Zeffy to make donating easy and secure. Use the form below to donate to the Submariners’ Advocacy Group.

Other Ways to Help

  • Volunteer: Join our team and help us support submariners and their families.
    • Researchers: Help us gather information and data to support our mission.
    • Report Writers: Help us create reports and white papers to share with key members of Congress and other organizations.
    • Technology Expertise: Website development, social media management, web application development, and more…
    • Public Relations: Help us spread the word about our mission and the needs of submariners.
  • Advocacy: Help us meet with key members of Congress and other organizations to advocate for submariners.
  • Spread the Word: Share our mission with your friends and family to increase awareness and support.

Thank you for your support!
