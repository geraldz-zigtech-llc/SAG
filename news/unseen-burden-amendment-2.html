The Unseen Burden, Amendment 2: Compounded Stressors

Following the release of our landmark report The Unseen Burden - Amendment 1, this amendment provides an analysis of how compounded stressors and other issues are associated with the toxic, hazardous, and radiation exposures.

Amendment 2 - Compounded Stressors and the Physiology of “Human Error”

  • Amendment 2PDF coming soon

    Comprehensive analysis of how multi-stressor environment and toxic exposure are a cumulative risk.

Key Focus Areas

This amendment expands on the original report and amendment 1 by providing:

  • Effects of cumulative risk exceeding human biological thresholds
  • Disruption of Circadian Rhythms and the Biological, Psychological, and Cognitive impacts
  • “Synergistic Trap”, how atmospheric conditions interact and magnify the impairment of a sleep-deprived brain
  • Matrix of Multi-Stressor Interactions
