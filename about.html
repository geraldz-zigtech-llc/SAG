About the Submariners’ Advocacy Group

The Submariners' Advocacy Group (SAG) is a nonprofit organization founded by U.S. Navy Submariners to serve, support, and advocate for every individual who has earned the right to wear the coveted Submarine Warfare Insignia—better known throughout the Fleet as the Dolphins.

Every member of SAG's leadership has completed the U.S. Navy's demanding submarine qualification process and understands firsthand the unique challenges, sacrifices, and lifelong bonds that define service in the Silent Service. Our shared experiences provide the perspective, credibility, and commitment necessary to advocate effectively on behalf of our submarine community.

Unlike traditional membership organizations, SAG recognizes that earning your Dolphins is membership enough. There are no applications, dues, or eligibility requirements beyond having been qualified in submarines. If you have served as a U.S. Navy Submariner, you are already part of the community we exist to represent.

SAG is dedicated to improving the lives of Submariners through advocacy, education, collaboration, and awareness. We work to ensure that the unique occupational and health concerns associated with submarine service receive the attention they deserve while building partnerships with healthcare professionals, researchers, veterans' organizations, policymakers, and other stakeholders who share our commitment to those who served beneath the sea.

Our mission extends beyond today's veterans. We advocate for active-duty Submariners, retirees, and future generations of undersea warriors by promoting research, supporting informed public policy, and encouraging greater understanding of the extraordinary service performed by the men and women of the United States Submarine Force.

The Silent Service has always been defined by professionalism, sacrifice, and quiet excellence. SAG exists to ensure that same commitment continues long after the watch has ended—serving as a trusted voice for our Brothers and Sisters of the submarine community wherever they may live.

Our Goals

The Submariners' Advocacy Group is committed to advancing the health, well-being, and recognition of the U.S. Navy submarine community through focused, long-term initiatives that create meaningful and lasting impact.

Advocate for the Submarine Community

Promote legislation, public policy, and institutional change that improve healthcare, benefits, recognition, and support for U.S. Navy Submariners, their families, and survivors.

Advance Research

Support the development of a nationwide Submariner registry, encourage scientific and medical research, and collaborate with leading academic and research institutions to better understand the long-term effects of submarine service.

Educate and Inform

Develop educational resources for Veteran Service Officers, healthcare professionals, policymakers, and the public to increase understanding of submarine operations, occupational exposures, and the unique challenges faced by those who served beneath the sea.

Build Strategic Partnerships

Establish collaborative relationships with veterans organizations, military commands, healthcare providers, universities, researchers, and government agencies to strengthen advocacy efforts and expand available resources.

Support Submariners and Their Families

Provide trusted information, guidance, and advocacy for Submariners, their families, and surviving family members while fostering a lifelong community built on shared service and mutual support.
