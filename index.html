Who We Are

The Submariners’ Advocacy Group (SAG) is a nonprofit organization founded by U.S. Navy Submariners to serve, support, and advocate for every individual who has earned the Submarine Warfare Insignia, known throughout the Fleet as the Dolphins.

If you have qualified in submarines, you are already part of the community we represent. There are no applications, dues, or eligibility requirements beyond earning your Dolphins.

Mission

Advocating for the health, recognition, and care of all U.S. Navy Submariners.
